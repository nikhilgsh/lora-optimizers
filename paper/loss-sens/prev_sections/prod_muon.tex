\section{Product Muon}\label{sec:product-muon}

We now return to the product structure of LoRA~\eqref{eq:lora}. First we adapt the Muon LMO~\eqref{eq:lmo} to be aware of the product structure, which yields Product Muon. 
Throughout, we set the LoRA scaling $\alpha=r$~\cite{loralearnsless, raschka}, so the merged weight is $\mat{W} = \mat{W}_0 + \mat{B}\mat{A}$. Consider a LoRA step that updates the two factors $\mat{A}$ and $\mat{B}$ as follows
\[
\mat{A} \leftarrow \mat{A} + \Delta\mat{A},
\qquad
\mat{B} \leftarrow \mat{B} + \Delta\mat{B}.
\]
Applying Muon naively would impose a spectral bound separately on $\Delta\mat{A}$ and $\Delta\mat{B}$, but what matters to the layer is the effective update of the product $\mat{B}\mat{A}$, which is not controlled solely by $\Delta\mat{A}$ and $\Delta\mat{B}$. Indeed, the update to the merged weight is, to first order,
\begin{equation} \label{eq:DeltaW-lin}
\Delta\mat{W} = (\mat{B} + \Delta\mat{B})(\mat{A} + \Delta\mat{A}) - \mat{B}\mat{A} \approx \mat{B} \Delta\mat{A} + \Delta\mat{B} \mat{A},
\end{equation}
from which we see that the spectral norm bound should be applied to this linearized product update. Substituting~\eqref{eq:DeltaW-lin} into the spectral LMO~\eqref{eq:lmo} gives the spectral constraint
\[
\norm{\mat{B} \Delta\mat{A} + \Delta\mat{B} \mat{A}}_2
\le \eta.
\]
The constraint couples the two unknowns $\Delta\mat{A}$ and $\Delta\mat{B}$, but we can decouple them with the upper bound using norm subadditivity:
\[
\norm{\mat{B} \Delta\mat{A} + \Delta\mat{B} \mat{A}}_2
\le
\norm{\mat{B} \Delta\mat{A}}_2 + \norm{\Delta\mat{B} \mat{A}}_2.
\]
Splitting the budget evenly between the two terms preserves the merged bound and gives the \emph{Product Muon} LMO
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
  \Delta\mat{A} &\in \argmin_{\Delta\mat{A}}~ \ip{\mat{G}_A, \Delta\mat{A}}
  \quad \textnormal{subject to}\quad
  \norm{\mat{B} \Delta\mat{A}}_2\le\eta/2, \\
  \Delta\mat{B} &\in \argmin_{\Delta\mat{B}}~ \ip{\mat{G}_B, \Delta\mat{B}}
  \quad \textnormal{subject to}\quad
  \norm{\Delta\mat{B} \mat{A}}_2\le\eta/2,
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:decoupled-lora-lmo}
\end{equation}
where the factor gradients are
\begin{equation}\label{e-grad-wrt-factors}
    \mat{G}_A := \nabla_A \mathcal{L}(\mat{W}_0+\mat{B}\mat{A}) = \mat{B}^\top \mat{G},
    \qquad
    \mat{G}_B := \nabla_B \mathcal{L}(\mat{W}_0+\mat{B}\mat{A}) = \mat{G} \mat{A}^\top.
\end{equation}
By \Cref{lem:precond-muon} with $\mat{P}=\mat{B}^\top \mat{B}$, $\mat{Q}=\mat{I}$, and $L=1$ for the $\mat{A}$ update, and symmetrically with $\mat{P}=\mat{I}$ and $\mat{Q}=\mat{A}\mat{A}^\top$ for the $\mat{B}$ update, the solutions to~\eqref{e-grad-wrt-factors} are given by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:product-muon-step}
\begin{aligned}
    \Delta\mat{A} &= -\tfrac{\eta}{2}\,(\mat{B}^\top \mat{B})^{-1/2}\,\msign\!\big((\mat{B}^\top \mat{B})^{-1/2} \mat{G}_A\big), \\
    \Delta\mat{B} &= -\tfrac{\eta}{2}\,\msign\!\big(\mat{G}_B (\mat{A} \mat{A}^\top)^{-1/2}\big)\,(\mat{A} \mat{A}^\top)^{-1/2}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
As in Muon, these factor updates are computed using momentum estimates of the gradients.
